# Calendar, attendance and ordinary services — Cycle 25

Explicit schedules use integer simulation civil minutes since 1940-01-01, derived
from the native clock. Gregorian date/year boundaries use the existing SDK-backed
civil mapping. This is a naive simulation calendar: historical time-zone changes,
holidays, worship days and tradition-specific practices are not inferred. Starts
must be future minute boundaries. One-shot and explicit day-interval schedules
support at most 366 nonoverlapping occurrences, 1–10080 minutes each.

Each gathering names a qualified office, supported ordinary purpose, staff wage
budget, attendee capacity, dates and basis. Worship, teaching, charity, meal and
visitation gatherings use compatible offices. Clinical and burial administration
use individual dispatch, rather than pretending a group ceremony supplies care or
moves a deceased body. Membership (maximum four explicit community links/person)
and attendance are separate intentions. Nearby homes, names and jobs imply neither.
A members-only event rechecks explicit membership while planning and crediting.

At most 64 explicit attendees per gathering are stored. The staff place also
counts against known premises capacity. Staff and attendees travel through the
same qualified street/door integrator. A missing worker, closure, blackout at
night, absent attendee, rest, care or travel prevents the corresponding output.
One staff native grant supplies a service minute; each present attendee consumes
its own shared grant. Meals and textile charity transfer finite venue inventory
once per attendee per occurrence. No food or money is generated by attendance.
Wages are reserved up front, paid per staff grant, and unused amounts refunded
once when the schedule ends or is cancelled. Missed minutes are not backfilled
outside their dated window. Repeated request keys return the same schedule.

Calendar records and attendee credits are pointer-free and covered by the city
digest. `cd_world_calendar_save/restore` provides a canonical little-endian,
CRC-checked component checkpoint bound to the current content, native instant and
complete city identity. Restore stages before publication and rejects corruption,
allocation failure, stale time and forged rollback without changing live state.
It is an idempotent same-instant component round-trip, **not a standalone whole-city
save**. Coordinated native/body/account/route checkpoint installation remains
Cycle 31; that coordinator must restore the full matching city generation. Native
fixtures establish batch/replay equivalence, year boundaries and component
round-trip without duplicated attendance, wages or transfers.
