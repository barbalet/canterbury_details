# Employment — Cycle 19

`cd_work.h` exposes employers, vacancies, primary/secondary assignments and shifts.
These are scenario configurations, distinct from land ownership, source building
identity, religious affiliation and ordinary occupation definitions. No automatic
hiring or fabricated historical staffing occurs. The shipped map still has no
reviewed walk links, doors or capacities; operational tests use labeled fixtures.

An employer needs known premises capacity and a named technical basis. Open jobs
cannot exceed its work capacity. IDs are stable; closing retains the vacancy
record. Regular employees must satisfy the catalogue age and skills. Apprentices
must be at least 16 and name a live, qualified adult with a regular job of the same
occupation at the same employer. Losing the last qualifying mentor assignment
cancels affected apprenticeships and refunds outstanding work reserves once.

Assignment requires a real position at a reviewed entrance endpoint or qualified
street node and a currently reachable route to the work entrance. It never moves
the body. Automatic commuting uses the existing CANTERBURY_SIM polyline and door
integrator; no island wandering or workplace teleport occurs. A secondary duty
must have a nonoverlapping daily shift. Start=end means a full-day technical
shift; overnight shifts wrap midnight. Assigned night workers receive daytime
sleep windows; fatigue, meals and care still take priority.

Schedules, wages, access keys and capacities require scenario configuration. They
are not measurements of 1940 employment. Closed employers/institutions suspend
work. Assignment does not create a productive task or assign a person's faith.
